Rambam - 3 Chapters a Day

Mechirah - Chapter 28, Mechirah - Chapter 29, Mechirah - Chapter 30

Mechirah - Chapter 28

1The following rules apply when a person tells a colleague: “I am selling you a parcel of earth1 fit to sow a kor.”2 If the land contains small hollows that are ten handbreadths deep3 even if they do not contain water,4 or rocks that are ten handbreadths high, they are not included in the above measure.5 The rationale is that a person does not want to pay money for one parcel of land and have it appear as two or three parcels.אהָאוֹמֵר לַחֲבֵרוֹ 'בֵּית כּוֹר עָפָר אֲנִי מוֹכֵר לָךְ', וְהָיוּ בְּתוֹךְ הַשָּׂדֶה גֵּאָיוֹת קְטַנִּים עֲמוּקִים עֲשָׂרָה טְפָחִים, אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁאֵין בָּהֶן מַיִם, אוֹ סְלָעִים גְּבוֹהִים עֲשָׂרָה טְפָחִים - אֵין נִמְדָּדִין בִּכְלַל הַבֵּית כּוֹר; שֶׁאֵין אָדָם רוֹצֶה לִתֵּן אֶת מְעוֹתָיו בְּמָקוֹם אֶחָד, וְנִרְאִין לוֹ כִּשְׁנַיִם אוֹ כִּשְׁלוֹשָׁה מְקוֹמוֹת.
The purchaser acquires these rocks and hollows as part of the parcel of land fit to sow a kor without paying for them.6וְלוֹקֵחַ אֵלּוּ הַגֵּאָיוֹת וְהַסְּלָעִים בִּכְלַל הַבֵּית כּוֹר, בְּלֹא דָּמִים.
If the hollows or the rocks are smaller than ten handbreadths,7 they are measured together with the remainder of the field.הָיוּ פָחוֹת מִכָּאן, נִמְדָּדִין עִמָּהּ.
2When does the above apply? When together, the area of all the rocks and the hollows was no more than the area necessary to sow four kabbim,8 and was contained within an area where at least five9 kabbim could be sown,10 and was contained within the majority of the field.11בבַּמֶּה דְּבָרִים אֲמוּרִים? בְּשֶׁלֹּא הָיָה בָּהֶן אֶלָא בֵּית אַרְבָּעָה קַבִּין, וְהָיוּ הָאַרְבָּעָה קַבִּין מֻבְלָעִין בְּתוֹךְ חֲמֵשֶׁת קַבִּין וּמֻבְלָעִין בְּרֻבָּהּ שֶׁל שָּׂדֶה.

Mechirah - Chapter 29

1There are three types of individuals whose purchase is not considered a binding purchase,1 nor is their sale considered a binding sale according to Scriptural Law:2 a deaf mute,3 a mentally incapable or emotionally unstable individual, and a minor.אשְׁלוֹשָׁה אֵין מִקָּחָן מִקָּח וְאֵין מִמְכָּרָן מִמְכָּר, דִּין תּוֹרָה: הַחֵרֵשׁ, וְהַשּׁוֹטֶה, וְהַקָּטָן.
Our Sages, however, ordained that a deaf mute and a minor may buy and sell in order to guarantee their livelihood.אֲבָל חֲכָמִים תִּקְּנוּ שֶׁיִּהְיֶה הַחֵרֵשׁ וְהַקָּטָן נוֹשֵׂא וְנוֹתֵן בַּמִּטַּלְטְלִין וּמַעֲשָׂיו קַיָּמִין, מִשּׁוּם כְּדֵי חַיָּיו.
2How does a deaf person buy and sell? Both a deaf mute and a deaf person4 who can speak may buy and sell through gestures.5 This applies with regard to movable property, but not with regard to landed property.6בהַחֵרֵשׁ כֵּיצַד נוֹשֵׂא וְנוֹתֵן? חֵרֵשׁ שֶׁאֵינוֹ לֹא שׁוֹמֵעַ וְלֹא מְדַבֵּר, אוֹ שֶׁמְּדַבֵּר וְאֵינוֹ שׁוֹמֵעַ כְּלוּם - מוֹכֵר וְלוֹקֵחַ בִּרְמִיזָה בַּמִּטַּלְטְלִין, אֲבָל לֹא בַּקַרְקַע.
Moreover, even with regard to movable property, his deeds are not binding until he has been tested many times7 and the matter is considered thoroughly by the court.8וְאַף בַּמִּטַּלְטְלִין, לֹא יִתְקַיְּמוּ מַעֲשָׂיו, עַד שֶׁבּוֹדְקִין אוֹתוֹ בְּדִיקוֹת רַבּוֹת, וּמִתְיַשְּׁבִין בַּדָּבָר.

Mechirah - Chapter 30

1When a person sells landed property or movable property to a colleague and someone acquires the property on the purchaser’s behalf without the purchaser’s knowledge,1 the option is given to the purchaser. If the purchaser desires to acquire the property, the seller cannot retract.2 If the purchaser does not desire to acquire the property, the property returns to its original owner.3אהַמּוֹכֵר שֶׁמָּכַר קַרְקַע אוֹ מִטַּלְטְלִין, וְזָכָה בָּהֶן לַלּוֹקֵחַ שֶׁלֹּא מִדַּעְתּוֹ - יַד הַלּוֹקֵחַ עַל הָעֶלְיוֹנָה: וְאִם רָצָה לּוֹקֵחַ, אֵין הַמּוֹכֵר יָכוֹל לַחֲזֹר בּוֹ; וְאִם לֹא רָצָה, תַּחְזֹר לִבְעָלֶיהָ.
Therefore, a deed of sale can be written for a seller even when the purchaser is not present.4לְפִיכָּךְ כּוֹתְבִין שְׁטָר לַמּוֹכֵר, אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁאֵין הַלּוֹקֵחַ עִמּוֹ.
The purchaser, however, is always required to pay the scribe’s fee.5 This applies even if a person sells his field because it is of inferior quality.6וּלְעוֹלָם הַלּוֹקֵחַ נוֹתֵן שְׂכַר הַסּוֹפֵר, וְאַפִלּוּ מָכַר שָׂדֵהוּ מִפְּנֵי רָעָתָהּ.
2Similarly, when a Canaanite7 servant buys or sells property, or whether he gives a present or a present is given to him, the option is given to the servant’s owner.8 If he desires to perpetuate the servant’s act, the servant’s acts are binding. If the master does not grant his consent, the servant’s acts are nullified entirely.9בוְכֵן הָעֶבֶד שֶׁמָּכַר אוֹ קָנָה אוֹ נָתַן מַתָּנָה, אוֹ נִתְּנָה לוֹ - יַד הָאָדוֹן עַל הָעֶלְיוֹנָה: אִם רָצָה לְקַיֵּם מַעֲשָׂיו, הֲרֵי אֵלּוּ קַיָּמִין; וְאִם לֹא רָצָה, בָּטְלוּ כָּל מַעֲשָׂיו.
