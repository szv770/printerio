Issurei Biah - Chapter 13

Issurei Biah - Chapter 13

1Israel entered the covenant with God1 with three acts: circumcision, immersion, and offering a sacrifice.אבִּשְׁלוֹשָׁה דְּבָרִים נִכְנְסוּ יִשְׂרָאֵל לַבְּרִית: בְּמִילָה, וּטְבִילָה, וְקָרְבָּן.
2Circumcision took place in Egypt, before the Paschal sacrifice, of which Exodus 12:48 says: “No uncircumcised person shall partake of it.” Moses our teacher circumcised the people.במִילָה הָיְתָה בְּמִצְרַיִם, כְּשֶׁנֶּאֱמַר "כָּל עָרֵל לֹא יֹאכַל בּוֹ" (שמות יב, מח). מָל אוֹתָם מֹשֶׁה רַבֵּנוּ.
For with the exception of the tribe of Levi, the entire people neglected the covenant of circumcision in Egypt.2 Regarding this, Deuteronomy 33:9 praises the Levites, saying: “They upheld Your covenant.”שֶׁכֻּלָּם בִּטְּלוּ בְּרִית מִילָה בְּמִצְרַיִם, חוּץ מִשֵּׁבֶט לֵוִי, וְעַל זֶה נֶאֱמַר "וּבְרִיתְךָ יִנְצֹרוּ" (דברים לג, ט).
3Immersion was performed in the desert before the Giving of the Torah, as Exodus 19:10 states: “Sanctify them today and tomorrow, and have them wash their garments.”גוּטְבִילָה הָיְתָה בַּמִּדְבָּר, קֹדֶם מַתַּן תּוֹרָה, כְּשֶׁנֶּאֱמַר "וְקִדַּשְׁתָּם הַיּוֹם וּמָחָר וְכִבְּסוּ שִׂמְלֹתָם" (שמות יט, י).
